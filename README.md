# ai-architecture-journal
An architect’s decision log: AI agents, knowledge bases, and the trade-offs

## About

I'm a Solution & Infrastructure Architect with 15+ years in large enterprises (aerospace, pharma, banking). This repo is my architecture lab notebook on AI agents: orchestration, knowledge bases, platforms, security. I test things in my home lab, compare options, measure, and write down what I chose and why, keeping in mind the constraints of both small and large organizations.

These are working notes: what I believe is a good way to approach a problem. Each decision fits my context and constraints at the time, and some may not hold at a larger scale. When that happens, I record a new decision rather than rewriting the old one. Feedback and other viewpoints are welcome.

The code for each experiment lives in its own repository.

## How to read an ADR

Each Architecture Decision Record (ADR) follows the same structure starting with a summary. 
The sections below it go deeper: criteria, options compared, tests and measurements, reasoning, consequences.

When a decision changes, the old ADR is not rewritten. It is marked "superseded by ADR-XXXX", so the evolution of my thinking stays visible.
